When you want dosa but forgot to soak the batter overnight, this **Instant Bread Dosa** is a lifesaver. It is incredibly crispy and tastes just like the real thing!

### Ingredients
- 4 slices of white or brown bread (edges removed)
- 1/2 cup Suji (Semolina)
- 1/4 cup Rice flour (for extra crispiness)
- 1/2 cup Curd (Yogurt)
- 1/2 cup Water
- Salt to taste

### Instructions
1. Tear the bread slices into pieces and add them to a blender.
2. Add the suji, rice flour, curd, water, and salt.
3. Blend until you get a smooth batter (similar to regular dosa batter consistency).
4. Heat a tawa (griddle), pour a ladle of batter, and spread it in a circular motion.
5. Drizzle a little oil or ghee around the edges and cook until crispy and golden.
6. Fold and serve hot with sambar and coconut chutney!
