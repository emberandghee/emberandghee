This **Vermicelli Rasmalai** is one of the most loved desserts on my channel. It gives you the rich, creamy flavor of traditional Rasmalai but with a unique texture from the roasted vermicelli.

### Ingredients
- 1/2 cup Roasted Vermicelli (Seviyan)
- 1 liter Full-fat Milk
- 1/2 cup Sugar (adjust to taste)
- A pinch of Saffron strands (Kesar)
- 1/2 tsp Cardamom powder
- 2 tbsp chopped nuts (Almonds, Pistachios)
- 1 tbsp Ghee

### Instructions
1. Heat ghee in a pan and lightly roast the vermicelli for 1 minute (even if it's pre-roasted) to release the aroma.
2. In a heavy-bottomed pan, bring the milk to a boil.
3. Add the saffron strands and simmer until the milk reduces to about 3/4th of its original volume.
4. Add the roasted vermicelli and cook on low heat until soft (about 8-10 minutes).
5. Add sugar, cardamom powder, and chopped nuts.
6. Let it simmer for another 2 minutes until it thickens slightly.
7. Serve chilled!
