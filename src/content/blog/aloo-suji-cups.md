These **Crispy Aloo Suji Breakfast Cups** are one of the most requested recipes on Ember and Ghee! They are incredibly satisfying, packed with vegetables, and very easy to make.

### Ingredients
- 1 cup Suji (Semolina)
- 2 boiled potatoes (Aloo), mashed
- 1/4 cup sweet corn
- 1/4 cup grated paneer
- 1/2 cup finely chopped bell peppers and onions
- 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
- Salt and spices to taste

### Instructions
1. Mix the suji, mashed potatoes, and a little water to form a thick batter. Let it rest for 10 minutes.
2. Mix all the veggies, paneer, and spices to create the filling.
3. Grease a small cup or appe pan. Pour a little batter, add a spoonful of filling, and cover with more batter.
4. Cook on low heat until golden brown and crispy on both sides.
5. Serve hot with green chutney!
